# Financial Performance and Portfolio Governance Requirements

## Purpose and users

This reporting process gives business management analysts, finance and operations managers, programme managers, and senior reviewers a single synthetic portfolio view. It shows approved budgets, actual expenditure, latest forecasts, material variances, risks, actions, and escalation needs.

## Reporting questions

1. What budget, actual, and forecast applies to each programme?
2. Which forecasts exceed the approved budget, and what changed since the previous forecast?
3. Which delivery and operational risks need management attention?
4. Which corrective actions are overdue or require escalation?

## Definitions and assumptions

Positive variances mean additional cost. Budget variance equals actual expenditure minus approved budget. Forecast variance equals forecast expenditure minus approved budget. Forecast movement equals current forecast minus previous forecast. Budget utilisation equals actual expenditure divided by approved budget. Remaining budget equals approved budget minus actual expenditure.

Forecast variance thresholds are configurable project assumptions: Green is at or below 2%, Amber is above 2% through 5%, and Red is above 5%. Risk score equals likelihood multiplied by impact. Green risk scores range from 1 to 5, Amber from 6 to 14, and Red from 15 to 25.

## Data ownership

Programme owners maintain programme master data. Finance controls maintain financial submissions. Risk and controls maintain risks and issues. Action owners maintain due dates and evidence. The Business Management Analyst runs the refresh, reviews exceptions, and records the refresh status.

All programme and financial values in this project are synthetic demonstrations. They are not company, employer, client, or JPMorgan Chase data.
